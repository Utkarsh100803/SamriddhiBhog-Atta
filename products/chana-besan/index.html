Samriddhi Bhog Chana Besan

Gram flour for chillas, pakoras, kadhi and ladoo · 500 g

Samriddhi Bhog Chana Besan is finely ground gram flour made from Bengal gram (chana), with no blending. It is chakki ground and has no added preservatives.

Use it for chilla, pakora batter, kadhi and ladoo — any recipe that needs the flavour of real besan.

How it’s made

Cleaned chana is ground on chakkis at our Dankuni mill, checked in our quality lab and packed in sealed 500 g packs.

Read how our chakki atta is made, or look inside our mill.

How to store it

  • Once opened, move the flour to a clean, dry, airtight container.
  • Keep it in a cool, dry place — away from the stove, the sink and direct sunlight.
  • Always use a dry spoon or dry hands.
  • Use it before the best-before date printed on the pack.
