Made with Samriddhi Bhog Sattu

Sattu Sharbat

Roasted chana flour whisked with chilled water, lemon and roasted cumin.

Prep
10 min
Cook
0 min
Serves
2

Ingredients

  • 4 tbsp (40 g) Samriddhi Bhog Sattu
  • 2 cups (500 ml) chilled water
  • 2 tbsp lemon juice
  • 1/2 tsp roasted cumin powder
  • 1/4 tsp black salt
  • Salt to taste
  • 1 tbsp chopped coriander
  • 1 small green chilli, finely chopped (optional)
The packSamriddhi Bhog Chana Sattu →

Method

  1. 1

    Whisk the sattu with about 1/2 cup of the water into a smooth, lump-free paste.

  2. 2

    Pour in the remaining water while whisking. Add lemon juice, cumin, black salt and salt.

  3. 3

    Stir in the coriander and chilli, taste, and adjust lemon or salt.

  4. 4

    Stir once more, divide between two glasses and serve immediately.

Kitchen notes

  • Making a paste first prevents dry pockets of sattu.
  • Sattu settles naturally — stir again if the drink stands.
