Certified Quality

Our products meet the world's most recognised food safety and quality standards.

Why Certifications Matter

Quality You Can Verify

At Heritage Flour Mills, certifications are not just paperwork — they represent our daily commitment to the highest standards of food safety, hygiene, and supply chain integrity. Every certificate is maintained with active audits and continuous factory-floor implementation.

These certifications are a prerequisite for our export business and reflect the trust that global buyers place in our products. For domestic clients, it means peace of mind — knowing that every bag of Heritage flour is safe, nutritious, and legal.

Our Credentials

All Active Certifications

🛡️
FSSC 22000
Issued by SGS India · Lic #IN25/00000805
Valid & Active

Food Safety System Certification 22000 — a globally recognised standard for food safety management systems, accepted by the GFSI (Global Food Safety Initiative).

Covers full production chain from wheat procurement to dispatch
Mandatory for many international supermarket buyers
Backed by continuous internal and external audits
☪️
HALAL Certification
Issued by Jamiat Ulama-e-Hind Halal Trust
Valid & Active

Our products comply with Islamic dietary law requirements, enabling exports to Muslim-majority markets across the Middle East, Africa, and Southeast Asia.

Essential for export to 57 OIC member countries
Annual factory audits by certified Halal inspectors
No cross-contamination with non-Halal products
🇮🇳
FSSAI License
Food Safety and Standards Authority of India
Valid up to Sep 2029

FSSAI is India's apex food safety regulator. Our license covers manufacture, storage, and distribution of wheat flour products throughout India.

Mandatory for all food manufacturers in India
Covers fortification requirements under FSSAI standards
Regular factory inspections by state food authority
🌾
APEDA Registration
Agricultural & Processed Food Products Export Development Authority
Active Exporter

APEDA registration is mandatory for all exporters of agricultural and processed food products from India. It enables access to government export promotion schemes.

Enables duty drawback and export incentives
Recognition under Government of India export schemes
Access to international trade fairs and buyer meets
📋
ISO 22000:2018
International Organization for Standardization
Valid & Active

ISO 22000 specifies requirements for a food safety management system — covering every organisation in the food supply chain from farm to table.

Internationally recognised by buyers globally
Integrated with HACCP principles and prerequisite programmes
Annual surveillance audits by accredited body
🏆
Star Export House
Directorate General of Foreign Trade, Govt. of India
Recognised Exporter

Recognition as a Star Export House by the Government of India reflects our proven track record of significant foreign exchange earnings through exports.

Priority customs clearance and faster port processing
Self-certification privileges for exports
Eligible for export promotion capital goods scheme
