Our Hygienic Process

From Grain to Your Kitchen — 100% Clean

Every bag of Samriddhi Bhog goes through a rigorous, hygienic process. We don’t cut corners — we clean them.

Wheat Selection & Soaking

Only premium-grade wheat is selected. It is properly soaked in clean water to soften the bran and remove surface dirt.

Thorough Washing & Drying

Wheat is washed multiple times and sun-dried naturally. No artificial heating — preserving all natural nutrients.

Cold-Press Stone Grinding

Traditional chakki grinding at low temperature retains the wheat’s natural oils, fibre, and aroma. No heat damage.

Lab Testing & Quality Check

Every batch is tested for moisture, ash content, and microbiological safety before packaging.

Sealed Hygienic Packaging

Workers wear hair caps, gloves, and uniforms. Atta is packed in multi-layer sealed bags to lock in freshness.
