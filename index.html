INSIDE OUR MILL

How We Make It

From golden grain to your kitchen — every step counts.

❧
01

Premium Grain Selection

Only the finest wheat varieties, sourced directly from trusted farmers.

02

Cleaning & Sorting

Grain is thoroughly cleaned, de-stoned, and sorted before milling.

03

Washing & Soaking

Washed and conditioned to ideal moisture for optimal milling.
