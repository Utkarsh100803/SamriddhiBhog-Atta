Our story

A Legacy Ground with Care Since 1950

In 1950, Shri Hanuman Prasad Agarwala began a humble Modi Khana shop with a small traditional chakki — where families came not just to buy atta, but for the trust of freshly ground wheat.

Every day, carefully selected wheat was ground in small quantities, carrying forward a simple belief: “Good atta is not manufactured, it is made with care.” As generations changed, the promise remained the same.

From one Modi Khana shop in 1950… to a 100 MT/day production capacity.
