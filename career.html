Why Heritage

A Career Worth Rising For

At Heritage Flour Mills, we believe that great products come from great people. We are a fast-growing, family-owned business that gives talented individuals real responsibility, meaningful work, and genuine opportunities to lead.

With 570 MT of production daily, 100+ clients, and exports spanning two continents, the scope of work here is as large as our ambitions. Whether you're a fresh graduate looking for your first role or an experienced professional seeking your next challenge — Heritage Flour Mills is where careers are built.

🌱 Entry-Level Welcome

We actively hire fresh graduates in food technology, commerce, engineering, and logistics. Full training provided.

📈 Fast Growth Culture

Performance is recognised and rewarded. Many of our current managers started as junior executives.

🤝 Family Values

We are a family business — we bring that warmth and trust into our workplace culture every day.
