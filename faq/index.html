About Samriddhi Bhog

What is Samriddhi Bhog?

What sizes does Samriddhi Bhog Atta come in?

+

Chakki Atta comes in 1 kg and 5 kg packs. Chana Besan, Wheat Dalia and Chana Sattu come in 500 g packs. All products are available in 50 kg bags for businesses.

What is the difference between atta and maida?

+

Atta is whole wheat flour — bran, germ and endosperm milled together. Maida is refined flour milled from the endosperm only.

Storage

How should I store atta?

+

After opening, keep it in a clean, dry, airtight container in a cool place away from heat and moisture, and always use a dry spoon.

How to store atta →

How long does atta stay fresh?

+

Use it by the best-before date printed on the pack, and store it airtight once opened.
