THE JOURNEY

From One Mill to a Trusted Brand

In 2004, we started with a simple belief: families deserve flour that is pure, fresh, and free from adulteration. What began as a single milling unit in Bihar has grown into a 365 lakh kgs/year production facility serving over 4000 businesses — kirana stores, restaurants, caterers, and sweet shops across the region.

We never chased scale at the cost of quality. Every batch is still processed with the same care — wheat washed and soaked before milling, tested for purity, and dispatched fresh. No preservatives, no maida blending, no shortcuts.

In 2024, we decided it was time to bring this purity directly to families. Samriddhi Bhog was born — not as a new product, but as a new promise: the same mill-fresh quality, now available at your doorstep via WhatsApp.

OUR MISSION

To give every Indian family access to flour as pure as what their grandmother ground at home.

We believe purity shouldn't be a luxury. It should be the standard. That's what Samriddhi Bhog stands for — flour you can trust, from a mill you can visit.
