Our Legacy — 20+ Years

🙏 A Family’s Promise of Purity

Samriddhi Bhog was born in 2004 with one belief — every household deserves flour that is pure, fresh, and made with honesty. No shortcuts, no chemicals, no compromise.

For over 20 years, we have been the trusted name for families who care about what goes into their rotis. From the wheat fields to your kitchen, every grain is handled with care and respect.

What started as a small stone mill has grown into a brand serving thousands of families and hundreds of dealers across Eastern India — yet we still mill with the same love and dedication as day one.

Our Capabilities

How We Make Your Atta

Wheat Soaking & Washing

Wheat is soaked in clean water to soften the bran, then washed multiple times to remove dust, stones, and impurities. This step is skipped by most big brands.

Natural Sun Drying

After washing, wheat is sun-dried naturally — not machine-heated. This preserves the grain’s natural taste, aroma, and nutritional value.

Cold-Press Stone Milling

Traditional chakki stone grinding at low temperature. Unlike roller mills that generate heat and destroy nutrients, our process keeps the wheat’s natural oils and fibre intact.

Lab Testing

Every batch is tested for moisture content, ash value, gluten strength, and microbiological safety. Only lab-approved batches proceed to packaging.

Sealed Packaging

Multi-layer laminated pouches lock in freshness. Packed by workers in hair caps, gloves, and sanitized uniforms. No human touch on the atta itself.

Home Delivery

From our mill to your door. No middlemen, no market runs. Fresh atta delivered within hours of packing. Just order on WhatsApp.

Hygiene & Safety

Contactless & Hygienic Manufacturing

From wheat intake to sealed packaging, our manufacturing process minimizes human contact. Every step is monitored, sanitized, and FSSAI-compliant.

  • Workers wear hair caps, gloves, and full sanitized uniforms at all times
  • Wheat moves through closed conveyors — no open handling
  • Automated packaging with no manual touching of finished flour
  • Daily deep cleaning of all machinery and surfaces
  • Regular third-party lab testing for microbiological safety
  • FSSAI registered and fully compliant across all products
