Export Leadership

Eastern India's#1 Flour Exporter

Heritage Flour Mills is the largest exporter of wheat grains and wheat flour from Eastern India. We export to several countries across Far East Asia and Africa, drawing on over two decades of international trade experience.

Our mill sits just 40 km from Kolkata Port — giving us the fastest cargo transit time among all Eastern India competitors. We handle both container and break-bulk shipments, and are recognised by all major shipping lines for best-rate access across our trade lanes.

We are officially recognised as a Star Export House by the Government of India with certifications including SGS-certified FSSC 22000, HALAL, APEDA, FIEO, and FSSAI — ticking every box for international buyers.
