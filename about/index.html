The journey

From a Modi Khana to a modern mill

In 1950, Shri Hanuman Prasad Agarwala began a humble Modi Khana shop with a small traditional chakki — where families came not just to buy atta, but for the trust of freshly ground wheat.

Every day, carefully selected wheat was ground in small quantities, carrying forward a simple belief: “Good atta is not manufactured, it is made with care.” As generations changed, the promise remained the same.

Taking forward his father’s values, his son transformed this traditional chakki into a modern flour mill — combining age-old chakki wisdom with advanced technology. Today, Samriddhi Bhog products are manufactured at Shree Hanuman Atta Mills Pvt. Ltd., where grains and pulses go through a complete process of:

  • Careful Wheat Selection
  • Advanced Cleaning & Sorting
  • Stone Chakki Processing
  • Lab Testing & Automated Packing, ensuring consistent quality

With stone chakkis, a production capability of 100 MT/day, and modern quality systems — every pack carries the same purity that started in a small Modi Khana more than six decades ago.

We never chased scale at the cost of quality. Every batch is still processed with the same care — grains washed before milling, tested for purity, and dispatched fresh. No preservatives, no maida blending, no shortcuts.

In 2024, we decided it was time to bring this purity directly to families. Samriddhi Bhog was born — not as a new product, but as a new promise: the same mill-fresh quality, now available at your nearest kirana stores.

From one Modi Khana shop in 1950… to a 100 MT/day production capacity.

Samriddhi Bhog combines the age-old goodness of stone chakki grinding with advanced cleaning, controlled processing, and hygienic automated packing — bringing wholesome flour to every household.
